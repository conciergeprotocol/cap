## Using the assets

- Use the files as they are. Do not recolor the mascot, change the cap, or redraw it.
- Leave space around the mascot, and place it on a light or plain background so the navy jacket stays visible.
- Write the name as "Concierge Agent Protocol" or "CAP". Say "implements CAP" or "compatible with CAP" when your site passes the conformance check.
- Do not use the mascot or mark to suggest that a product is official, certified, or endorsed by the CAP maintainers, and do not put them in a product name, company name, or logo.
